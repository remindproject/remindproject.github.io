Returns to Effort and Growth Mindset

Hello! We're a team of social scientists from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (UC3M). In REMind we look at how much effort really matters for getting ahead, and what growth mindset has to do with it.

Publications Meet the team

Overview

Is getting ahead about talent, hard work or luck? We know a lot about how ability pays off, but much less about effort. We want to find out what effort is really worth, and how growth mindset (believing you can improve with practice) fits in.

To do it, we'll run a new online panel in Spain, test a short growth mindset course and dig into data on teenagers around the world. The goal: see who gets rewarded for trying hard, and how we can help more people get there.

Objectives

🎯

Measure the payoff of effort

Does trying hard pay off? And does it depend on age, gender or family background?

🔬

Link mindset and effort

Does believing you can improve make teens try harder and do better at school?

📊

Test a simple fix

Can a short online course boost effort and results for young adults?
