Returns to Effort and Growth Mindset

Hello! In this website you will find everything on the REMind project, where we look to explore the role of effort in shaping equality of opportunity, the economic returns to effort and how having a growth or fixed mindset can impact people's lives.

Publications Meet the team

Overview

Is getting ahead in Spain about talent, hard work or luck? We know a lot about how ability pays off, but much less about effort. We want to find out what effort is really worth, and how growth mindset (the belief that you can improve with practice) fits in.

To do it, we'll follow young and older adults in Spain over time, test a short online growth mindset course, and analyze data on teenagers from around the world. The goal: see who gets rewarded for trying hard, and how we can help more people get there.

Objectives

🎯

Measure the payoff of effort

Does trying hard pay off? And does it depend on age, gender or family background?

🔬

Link mindset and effort

Does believing you can improve make teens try harder and do better at school?

🎓

Can a growth mindset be taught?

We'll run an online course to boost people's growth mindset, then test whether it made a difference to their effort and results.
